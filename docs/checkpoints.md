## Key methodological correction

Airport passenger numbers are not treated as direct proof of tourist nationality or client potential.

They are only used as an access proxy.

Important caution:

- Copenhagen may be a transfer / hub route, so it should not automatically be treated as strong Danish demand.
- London / UK is a large route, but includes strong Polish family / diaspora travel.
- Norway routes may also include Polish work / family travel, not only tourism.
- Therefore, route volume must be interpreted together with visitor intent, wallet, aesthetic fit and local market language.

## Sweden: local tattoo market language

Initial desk research on Stockholm and Gothenburg tattoo studios suggests that the visible local market strongly communicates:

- black / grey
- fine line
- microrealism
- minimal / delicate tattoos
- blackwork
- realism
- custom work
- technical precision
- controlled visual language

Important interpretation:

This is not only a color preference.  
It may be a cultural code of:

- safety
- timelessness
- maturity
- control
- precision
- subtlety
- quality
- low visual risk

Working market read:

Swedish tattoo communication appears to be controlled, subtle, refined, intentional and often black/grey-based, with color more likely to function as an intentional accent than as a loud default.

No final content conclusion has been made yet.

## Finland

Finland may be the strongest aesthetic fit for Nadia.

Reasons:

- forest / nature associations
- storybook feeling
- small creatures
- soft nostalgia
- emotional illustration culture
- Moomin-related cultural sensitivity and visual memory

Important IP caution:

Moomins / Muminki are protected characters.  
They should not be used as a commercial campaign theme, ad hook, flash collection or landing page asset without license.

Safe direction:

- Nordic storybook
- forest creatures
- soft creatures
- tiny magical tattoos
- nature-connected small tattoos
- original Nadia creatures

Avoid:

- “Moomin tattoos”
- direct Moomin characters
- Little My / Snufkin / Moomintroll references
- paid promotion of recognizable Moomin-inspired designs

| soft | gentle, emotional, quiet, non-aggressive |

| care | care for body, skin, process and person |
| story | personal meaning, memory, small narrative |
| ritual | tattoo as experience, not only service |
| wearable | something that lives well with body and style |

Important note:

“Slow” is a key Nordic code.
